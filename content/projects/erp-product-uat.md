# Modular ERP — Product and UAT Collaboration

## Overview

I contributed product reviews and User Acceptance Testing to a modular ERP for service-oriented businesses. The product covers customer and project management, scheduling, inventory, expenses, invoicing, payroll, and administration.

## Attribution

My role was product collaboration, workflow review, testing, and User Acceptance Testing. Rasmad Ibnu and the Naestra team developed the software in the private repository. I did not author the application code described here.

## Context

An ERP workflow can span several screens and user roles. During UAT, I checked whether those steps matched the expected business process, including permissions, validation, and the result visible to the user.

## My Role

- Reviewed business workflows and product behavior.
- Exercised end-to-end scenarios from an operational user's perspective.
- Reported usability, validation, navigation, and workflow issues.
- Checked whether expected outcomes matched product requirements.
- Contributed feedback for onboarding, invoicing, dashboard, and operational flows.
- Distinguished implementation defects from product decisions requiring clarification.

## UAT Approach

```text
Business scenario
  -> prerequisites and test data
  -> user journey execution
  -> expected-versus-actual comparison
  -> issue classification and evidence
  -> retest after correction
  -> acceptance or follow-up decision
```

## Results

I turned business expectations into test scenarios, compared expected and actual behavior, and reported issues for the engineering team to review and retest.

## Limitations

- This is not presented as my software implementation.
- Private product architecture, customer configuration, screenshots, and source code are excluded.
- UAT evidence and production status are not published without team approval.

## Technology Context

The private product uses a TypeScript monorepo with Next.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL, background processing, reusable modules, and role-aware multi-tenant workflows.
