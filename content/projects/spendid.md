# Spendid

## Overview

Spendid is a collaborative finance SaaS project for personal, household, and small-business use. I contributed ideas from KasMate and reviewed workflows as the product expanded to shared workspaces, invitations, subscriptions, and invoicing.

## Attribution

My contribution covers product ideas, workflow input, and review. Rasmad Ibnu and the Naestra team primarily developed the implementation in the current repository. I do not claim that implementation as my sole engineering work.

## Context

Shared and business finances introduce questions about invitations, permissions, workspace ownership, and billing. My review work considered how those requirements fit the transaction, budget, debt, and invoice flows.

## My Role

- Contributed product perspective derived from building KasMate.
- Discussed finance workflows and expected user behavior.
- Participated in product review and collaborative direction.
- Evaluated how personal-finance concepts could expand into shared and business workspaces.

## Product Architecture

At a high level, the product uses a Next.js frontend, a NestJS API, PostgreSQL, a background worker, and Redis-backed jobs. It includes multi-workspace access, finance modules, billing concepts, and responsive interfaces.

## Results

This collaboration gave me a way to apply lessons from KasMate to shared and business finance workflows. My contribution on this page is product input and review; the credited team owns the implementation work.
